RepForge Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026 • Effective Date: September 11, 2026

RepForge is an open-source, privacy-first workout logging application developed by Devasy Patel. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is treated when using the RepForge mobile app.

1. Core Principle: Local-First Architecture

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide personal identity details to use RepForge.
  • Local Storage: All workout sessions, routines, personal records (PRs), analytics, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database.
  • No Developer Servers: We do not operate developer servers or centralized databases. Your workout data stays securely on your device.

2. Health Connect Integration (Android Health Connect)

RepForge integrates with Android Health Connect to provide workout synchronization and recovery insights. This feature requires your explicit runtime consent.

Permissions Requested

Permission Purpose
WRITE_EXERCISE Writes completed RepForge workouts to Health Connect for other apps to see.
READ_EXERCISE Reads previous workout sessions to maintain consistent exercise history.
READ_SLEEP Reads sleep duration and stages to compute your daily readiness score.
READ_HEART_RATE / READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE Reads resting heart rate metrics to evaluate physiological strain and recovery.
READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY Reads HRV data to compute physiological readiness for training.
Health Connect Privacy Guarantees:
  • Health Connect data is processed strictly on-device. It is never transmitted to external servers.
  • Health data is never sold, licensed, or shared with data brokers, advertisers, or credit/insurance agencies.
  • You can revoke or modify these permissions at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect.

3. Optional Generative AI Features (AI Coach)

RepForge includes an optional AI Coach and Routine Optimizer powered by Google Gemini API:

  • Completely Optional: This feature is dormant unless you explicitly opt in and enter your personal Gemini API key under Settings → AI Settings.
  • Direct HTTPS Connection: Prompts and requested workout context are transmitted directly from your device to Google Gemini API servers over encrypted HTTPS. RepForge operates no intermediary server.
  • Data sent to Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

  • No Ads: RepForge is 100% ad-free.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No tracking SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) are included in RepForge.
  • Offline Fonts: Typography (Geist / GeistMono) is embedded directly into the app binary, avoiding runtime network font requests.

5. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

  • Data Portability: Export a full JSON backup of all your workouts at any time in Settings → Backup & Restore → Export.
  • Complete Deletion: Clear your data anytime inside the app, clear app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Since data is local, uninstalling permanently deletes all stored data.
